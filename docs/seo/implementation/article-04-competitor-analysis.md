## Content gaps found

The competitor set generally covers one of three angles:

1. broad "what is WhatsApp commerce" definitions;
2. ecommerce journey or conversational buying;
3. catalog/policy-specific how-to content.

The gaps for a TalkWagon article are:

- explain WhatsApp commerce as an operational workflow, not just a feature;
- separate WhatsApp Business App use from WhatsApp Business Platform workflows;
- explain catalog-assisted commerce versus traditional ecommerce checkout;
- include team-inbox responsibilities and human handoff;
- add safe payment and sensitive-information guidance;
- avoid fake statistics, fake case studies, and guaranteed outcome claims;
- link to `/use-cases/sales` as the commercial page instead of competing with it.

## Recommended article length

Competitor coverage is broad enough that a thin 1,200-word explainer would not be useful. Article 04 should be approximately 3,000–3,600 useful words so it can cover:

- definition;
- customer journey;
- catalog/shop concepts;
- app versus platform;
- CRM/team responsibilities;
- automation and handoff;
- policy and payment safety;
- practical business examples;
- mistakes;
- launch checklist;
- FAQs and authoritative sources.

## Cannibalization conclusion

V2.1 maps `whatsapp commerce` as a strict opportunity and secondary keyword for `/use-cases/sales`. Article 04 is acceptable only if it stays educational and supports `/use-cases/sales`.

The final Article 04 implementation:

- does not replace `/use-cases/sales`;
- links to `/use-cases/sales` as the commercial next step;
- avoids sales-page CTA duplication;
- covers informational-commercial explainer intent;
- does not introduce a separate "WhatsApp sales" target.

## Why this is the correct next article

The legacy V2 plan names `whatsapp automation guide` as Article 06, but both V2.1 and V2.2 resolve that topic into `/features/automation`. Publishing the legacy article would create a known cannibalization conflict. The V2.2 article-batch instructions require a numeric keyword that is not already owned by a commercial page.

`delightchat alternative` is the strongest unused English future article/comparison candidate recorded after Batch 04:

- it has numeric historical volume and KD;
- it is explicitly recorded as a future article or comparison candidate;
- no TalkWagon route currently owns this keyword;
- it is different from `/wati-alternative` because it evaluates a different product and a Shopify/omnichannel-versus-WhatsApp-first CRM decision;

## Cannibalization conclusion

- Articles 01–03 cover greeting messages, away messages, and quick replies.

Article 06 may link to these pages but must not reposition itself as their primary ranking asset. Cannibalization risk is low when the H1, title, canonical, body, and internal anchor strategy remain focused on `delightchat alternative`.

## Evidence-led angle

Most current comparison pages are vendor-authored listicles. Several contain claims contradicted by DelightChat's current official pages, including outdated statements about mobile apps, channels, pricing, and capabilities. Article 06 should instead:

1. date every price observation;
2. use DelightChat's official pricing/help center for DelightChat facts;
3. use TalkWagon's public product and pricing pages for TalkWagon facts;
4. distinguish Shopify-native omnichannel support from WhatsApp-first CRM workflows;
5. state where either product is the more natural fit;
6. avoid claiming that TalkWagon replaces features it does not currently advertise;
7. remind readers that Meta/WhatsApp charges are separate from CRM subscription pricing.

## Planned length and structure

Recommended length: 2,800–3,600 words. Competitor pages commonly use long listicles but often omit a verification method. A complete TalkWagon article should include:

1. direct answer and decision summary;
2. what DelightChat currently offers;
3. why a team may evaluate alternatives;
4. TalkWagon versus DelightChat comparison table with evidence date;
5. inbox and channel model;
6. Shopify/ecommerce workflows;
7. CRM contacts, pipelines, and follow-up;
8. broadcasts and Meta templates;
9. automation, flows, and AI knowledge answers;
10. team access and administration;
11. pricing-model and total-cost checklist;
12. migration checklist;
13. “choose DelightChat if / choose TalkWagon if” guidance;
14. FAQs;
15. dated sources and non-affiliation notice.

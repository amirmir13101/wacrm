# Article 07 Image Prompts

All images were generated for the managed draft `best-whatsapp-crm`. The production assets use a 16:9, 1600x900 composition; TalkWagon's dark forest-green, emerald, amber, and white visual system; fictional US business data; and no competitor logos or endorsement claims.

## 1. Comparison workspace hero

Create a premium editorial SaaS visual for “Comparing WhatsApp CRM Software.” Show an evaluation workspace combining inbox, contacts, pipeline, automation, campaigns, a buyer checklist, and anonymous options. Use readable fictional US data and a tasteful TalkWagon wordmark. No rankings, competitor logos, endorsements, watermarks, or fabricated claims.

## 2. Buyer requirements workshop

Create a professional weighted CRM requirements scorecard for inbox, contact context, pipeline, automation, campaigns, permissions, and reporting. Show must-have and nice-to-have controls, weights, notes, approvals, and a neutral project team. Do not declare a winner.

## 3. Evaluation framework

Show four stages: Define Requirements, Shortlist Platforms, Test Real Workflows, and Compare Total Cost. Include a neutral Platform A-D matrix with Meets, Partial, and Test Needed states and a decision note explaining that fit depends on workflow and requirements.

## 4. Shared inbox and contact context

Show an unassigned customer conversation being assigned to a fictional agent, an internal note, priority, customer profile, and complete conversation history. Add checklist items for ownership, visible context, internal collaboration, and handoff continuity.

## 5. Sales pipeline workflow

Show a conversation becoming a contact and moving through New Lead, Qualified, Proposal, Negotiation, and Won stages. Include owner, next task, activity history, and a buyer checklist without performance claims.

## 6. Broadcast and consent controls

Show an approved template, opted-in audience, exclusion list, frequency guard, preflight checklist, queued campaign processing, and traceable statuses. Include a responsible-delivery note and avoid guaranteed-delivery claims.

## 7. Automation and human handoff

Show connected trigger, intent, wait, condition, tag, approved-knowledge AI, confidence, human handoff, and reporting nodes. Beside it, show context-preserving transfer to a fictional agent.

## 8. Total cost and decision checklist

Show blank US-dollar fields for subscription, messaging charges, seats, onboarding, integrations, AI, support, and migration. Pair the cost worksheet with a 30-day trial scorecard and anonymous Platform A-C columns. No winner, ranking, or fabricated prices.

## Review outcome

Two rejected candidates were not used:

- one introduced a fabricated TalkWagon winner and invented three-year costs;
- one used the wrong currency for the US-targeted article.

Both were replaced before optimization.

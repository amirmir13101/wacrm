# Article 05 Competitor Analysis

## Topic

`How to Integrate WhatsApp with a CRM`

## Primary keyword

`integrating whatsapp with crm`

## Competitor/source patterns reviewed

| Source | Common coverage | Gap TalkWagon fills |
|---|---|---|
| Wati | Benefits of WhatsApp CRM integration and product-led connector framing | More neutral integration-route comparison and stronger data-mapping guidance |
| Respond.io | WhatsApp CRM setup, API/connectivity, team inbox concepts | Clearer pre-launch test checklist and failure-state handling |
| Picky Assist | Step-based WhatsApp CRM integration concepts | More practical ownership, opt-in, and handoff guidance |
| LeadSquared | CRM + WhatsApp for sales and lead handling | More detailed webhooks/data/automation explanation for SMB operators |
| SleekFlow | Omnichannel/conversational commerce CRM positioning | More explicit distinction between Business App, provider bridge, WhatsApp-native CRM, and custom API |
| Kommo | CRM integration and WhatsApp sales use cases | More balanced privacy/safety and operational checklist coverage |
| Zoho | Native CRM integration help and configuration context | Broader vendor-neutral explanation for teams that have not chosen a CRM route yet |

## Recommended content approach

The article should not be a thin “connect WhatsApp to CRM” checklist. It should be a decision guide that explains:

1. What integration really means.
2. When the WhatsApp Business App is enough.
3. Five integration routes and the trade-offs.
4. What data should sync.
5. What webhooks do in a CRM workflow.
6. Why ownership and team permissions matter.
7. How templates, opt-in, and outbound messages should be handled.
8. Where automation helps and where human handoff is safer.
9. How to test the integration before launch.
10. Common mistakes to avoid.

## Cannibalization decision

Safe, with boundaries.

The article supports the broad homepage and feature pages, but it should not target the generic `whatsapp crm` head term directly. The article links to:

- `/features/team-inbox`
- `/features/automation`
- `/features/flows`
- `/use-cases/sales`
- `/pricing`

This keeps the article informational and lets the commercial pages remain the conversion targets.

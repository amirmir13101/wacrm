## Migration checklist before switching from SleekFlow

Do not switch tools only because a competitor page looks attractive. Migration should be deliberate.

### 1. Export and audit contacts

Check names, phone numbers, country codes, tags, lifecycle stage, consent status, source, and any fields your agents use while replying. Bad contact data makes every CRM look worse than it is.

### 2. Review templates

List every approved WhatsApp template, language, category, variable, sample value, and actual use case. If your campaigns depend on templates, do not leave this until the final day.

### 3. Map inbox ownership

Write down how conversations are assigned today and how they should work after migration. Include managers, agents, escalation rules, and fallback ownership.

### 4. Rebuild critical automations first

Do not rebuild every old flow at once. Start with the workflows that protect revenue or customer experience: greeting, qualification, support routing, broadcast reply handling, abandoned lead follow-up, and human handoff.

### 5. Test broadcasts with a small audience

Send a small internal or low-risk campaign before moving a full audience. Confirm personalization, delivery, failures, opt-outs, and replies.

### 6. Train agents on the daily workflow

Agents need to know where to reply, how to assign, how to update contacts, when to use templates, and when to escalate.
